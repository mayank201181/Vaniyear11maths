# School objectives (Tanglin Trust — Maths 27G, Year 11)

Transcribed from the teacher's OneNote "Student Objectives" / "H+ Student Objectives" pages.
Colour key in the school notebook: **[F]** yellow = foundation/core, **[H]** blue = Higher,
**[H+]** green = Higher Plus extension (Further-Maths flavour). Every objective below must be
taught in the guide and practised in the questions of the topic shown in brackets.

## Unit 1 — Expanding & factorising; substitution (→ `expand-factorise`, `functions`)
- [F] Can I expand and fully simplify two brackets?
- [F] Can I factorise expressions in the form x² + bx + c?
- [H] Can I expand and fully simplify up to three brackets?
- [H] Can I factorise quadratic expressions where a ≠ 1?
- [H] Can I complete the square?
- [H+] Can I expand and factorise harder algebraic expressions?
- [F] Can I substitute positive and negative integers, decimals and fractions in expressions and formulae?
- [H] Can I explain function notation? Calculate f(x) where x is given? Find x given f(x) and the value of f(x)?

## Unit 2 — Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio (→ `fractions-percentages`, `ratio-proportion`)
- [F] Can I 'show how' to use four operations with fractions including mixed numbers without a calculator?
- [H] Can I fully simplify an algebraic fraction by finding common factors? …by factorising?
- [H] Can I multiply/divide and fully simplify algebraic fractions? Add/subtract and fully simplify algebraic fractions?
- [F] Can I identify terminating and recurring decimals? Convert fractions to decimals by division?
- [H] Can I prove that a recurring decimal and fraction are equivalent using algebra?
- [F] Percentage increase/decrease with or without a multiplier; reverse percentages; compound interest and depreciation.
- [H] Can I use repeated percentage change?
- [F] Ratio: simplest form; divide quantities into a ratio; compare concentrations; problems with scale drawings, value for money, recipes and exchange rates.

## Unit 3 — Indices & surds (→ `indices-surds`)
- [F] Index laws for multiplication and division with the same base; an index outside a bracket; negative indices as reciprocals; standard form worded problems.
- [H] Can I simplify fractional indices? Solve equations involving indices by changing the base?
- [H+] Can I solve harder indices equations such as x^(5/2) = 32 (FM only)?
- [H] Surds: collect like surds; multiplication and division rules; multiply out two brackets containing mixed surds; rationalise a simple surd denominator; rationalise a mixed surd denominator.

## Unit 4 — Length, area & volume (→ `mensuration`, `similarity-congruence`, `number-bounds`, `ratio-proportion`)
- [F] Area and perimeter of semicircles. [H] Area and perimeter of sectors.
- [F] Surface area of cuboids, triangular prisms and cylinders. [H] Surface area of spheres and cones.
- [F] Pythagoras to find missing lengths. [H] Solve problems using upper and lower bounds.
- [F] Volume of prisms incl. cuboids, triangular prisms and cylinders. [H] Volume of cones and spheres.
- [F] Missing lengths in similar shapes. [H] Convert between units of area and volume; missing areas and volumes in similar shapes.
- [F] Formulae in compound measures, e.g. speed or density.

## Unit 5 — Solving equations (→ `solving-equations`, `quadratic-equations`)
- [F/H] Solve a pair of simultaneous equations with two variables. [H+] Solve a trio of simultaneous equations with three variables.
- [F] Change the subject where the subject appears once. [H] …appears twice and I need to factorise.
- [F] Solve a quadratic with no factorising required; by factorising.
- [H] By completing the square; using the formula; a pair of equations where one is linear and one quadratic; equations involving algebraic fractions.
- [H+] Solve harder quadratics including 'disguised' quadratic equations.

## Unit 6 — Statistics (→ `statistics`)
- [F] Mean, median, mode and range of small discrete data sets. [H] Interquartile range from data sets.
- [F] Mean, median, mode and range from a discrete frequency table; estimate the range from a grouped table; modal and median class; estimate the mean from grouped data.
- [H] Construct and interpret cumulative frequency graphs incl. median and IQR; construct and interpret histograms for unequal class widths.

## Unit 7 — Graphs (→ `linear-graphs`, `graphs-of-functions`)
- [F] Equation of a straight line in the form y = mx + c; midpoint and distance between two coordinates using Pythagoras.
- [H] Point of intersection is the solution to simultaneous equations; use y − y₁ = m(x − x₁); gradients, intercepts and equations where y is implicit; equation of a normal to a line.
- [H+] Divide a line in a given ratio.
- [F] Plot a quadratic function using a table of values.
- [H] Solve simultaneous equations graphically where one is linear and the other quadratic or cubic; solutions of ax² + bx + c = 0 are the roots, solve graphically; recognise and plot quadratic, cubic and rational graphs; min/max of a quadratic by completing the square.
- [H+] Sketch a quadratic by completing the square showing intercepts, turning point and axis of symmetry; recognise and work with exponential functions.

## Unit 8 — Pythagoras & trigonometry (→ `pythagoras-trigonometry`, `further-trigonometry`)
- [F] Pythagoras' theorem for missing lengths. [H] With surds; in 3 dimensions. [H+] Pythagorean triples and simple multiples.
- [F] Right-angled trig for missing angles and lengths. [H] Bearings; angles of elevation and depression; in 3D. [H+] Exact values in 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangles.
- [H] Sine and cosine rule for any triangle; sine rule for the area of a triangle.
- [H] Recognise, sketch and use graphs of y = sin x, cos x, tan x to solve equations; effect of transforming trig graphs.
- [H+] Use the identities tan θ = sin θ / cos θ and sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; solve trig equations in given intervals.

## Unit 9 — Sets, probability, binomial (→ `sets-venn`, `probability`)
- [F] Venn diagrams for HCF and LCM of two or three numbers; set notation; construct Venn diagrams from contexts; identify regions using set notation and calculate probabilities.
- [F] Sample space diagrams; estimate probabilities from previous events; expected frequency; 'or' rule for mutually exclusive events; 'and' rule for independent events.
- [H] Tree diagrams for up to three events; use probability to construct and solve equations.
- [H+] Binomial expansion with simple binomial expressions to positive integer powers; the product rule for counting.

## Unit 10 — Angles & circle theorems (→ `angles-circle-theorems`)
- Basic angle facts; regular polygons (bronze/silver/gold); circle theorems (angle at centre, two radii, same segment, cyclic quadrilateral, tangents, alternate segment); proof of angle at the centre.

## Unit 11 — Proportion & sequences (→ `ratio-proportion`, `sequences`)
- [H] Set up and solve problems involving direct and inverse proportion.
- [F] nth term of an arithmetic sequence. [H] Use nth term = a + (n − 1)d; sum of the first n terms Sₙ.
- [H+] nth term of a sequence including fractional terms; nth term of a quadratic sequence; prove the limiting value of a sequence.

## Unit 12 — Inequalities & functions (→ `inequalities`, `functions`)
- [F] Represent and identify 'simple' inequalities on graphs.
- [H] 'Harder' regions defined by linear inequalities; solve quadratic inequalities and represent the solution set on a number line.
- [H] A function is a mapping between two sets; notation f(x) = … and f: x ↦ …; domain and range and excluded values; inverse f⁻¹(x); composite fg(x).

## Rest of Edexcel 4MA1 Higher (not in the screenshots, added for full coverage)
- `number-bounds`: prime factors, HCF/LCM, rounding, estimation, bounds.
- `calculus`: differentiation, tangents, turning points, kinematics (+ H+ normals).
- `vectors-transformations`: transformations and vector geometry.
- `angles-circle-theorems`: intersecting chords, constructions and loci.
